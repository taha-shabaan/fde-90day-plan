What the roadmap poster says

  • From X to FDE: FDE is not a beginner role — you are most probably already a Full-Stack Developer stepping into it.
  • Frontend + Backend: FDEs must be proficient in full-stack development, have high agency, and deliver full apps for customers.
  • DSA & System Design: the first gate in FDE interviews — you won't get to show your AI skills if you don't pass this round.
  • DevOps & Cloud: fluent in at least one major cloud platform and deploying infrastructure as code.
  • Production proof: FDEs are responsible for proving to customers that what they built won't break or go rogue in production.
